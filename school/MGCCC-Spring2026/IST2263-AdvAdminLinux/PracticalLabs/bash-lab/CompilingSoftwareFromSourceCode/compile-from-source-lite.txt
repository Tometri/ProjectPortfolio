1.The purpose of ./configure is to check for requirements, figure out system details, and generates final build files.
2.make reads instructions from the makefile and executes the build plan.
3. Apt tracks packages,installs/updates them, and uses packages which have typically been tested already. Make is more manual, manual upgrading and ensuring the right libraries and their versions eixist, new upgrades have to be reconfigured/installed/etc, and source builds may not be tested for the used distro.
